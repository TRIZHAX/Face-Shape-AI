      About

      A private, on-device face-shape and hairstyle advisor.

      How it works

      The app uses TensorFlow.js in your browser to detect facial landmarks, computes proportions, and classifies your face shape. Recommendations blend a curated hairstyle catalog with a light on-device learning loop that adapts to your likes, dislikes, and ratings.

      Privacy

      • No photos ever leave your device.
      • No accounts, cookies, analytics, or trackers.
      • Works fully offline after first load.

      Face shapes covered

      Oval, Round, Square, Rectangle, Oblong, Heart, Diamond, Triangle.

      Tech stack

      Static HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JS (ES6 modules), TensorFlow.js, MediaPipe FaceMesh, IndexedDB & LocalStorage.
